# Asset sources

[中文](SOURCE.zh-CN.md)

The table, cube and frame-marker assets come from NVIDIA's Isaac Sim 5.1 collection,
`Assets/Isaac/5.1/Isaac`. They reside in
`objects/seattle_lab_table/`, `objects/dex_cube/` and `objects/frame_prim/`.
Asset paths: [asset manifest](../benchmark/config/asset_manifest.json).

The pallet wood textures in `materials/pallet_wood/textures/` come from the same collection:
[color](https://omniverse-content-production.s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/Assets/Isaac/5.1/Isaac/Props/Pallet/Materials/Textures/FOF_Map_Palette_A_D.tga)
and [normal](https://omniverse-content-production.s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/Assets/Isaac/5.1/Isaac/Props/Pallet/Materials/Textures/FOF_Map_Palette_A_N.tga).
